Built different. Literally.

PDF Toolkit runs entirely in your browser. Every tool — merge, split, compress, convert, edit, sign, redact — uses JavaScript running on your own device. There is no backend server. There is no database.

Nothing you upload here is uploaded anywhere. When you open a PDF or image, it stays on your device. When you close the tab, it's gone. We literally have no way to see your files, because they never reach us.

How it actually works

PDF manipulation uses pdf-lib v1.17.1. PDF rendering for JPG export uses pdf.js v3.11. CV export uses html2pdf.js. Batch downloads are zipped client-side with JSZip. All libraries run locally in your browser — no API calls, no analytics on your documents.

You can verify this yourself: open the browser's DevTools → Network tab. You'll see no file uploads, ever. Just the initial page load.

Who it's for

Anyone who values their privacy. Students turning in assignments. Developers shipping contracts. Freelancers sending invoices. People who just don't want their tax documents sitting on someone else's server.

Honest limitations

We're not going to pretend to be perfect. Here's what we can't do:

• OCR (image → searchable text) requires heavy ML models — not available client-side yet.
• Password-protected PDFs with strong encryption may fail to open.
• Compression ratios depend on your file — scanned images compress more than text PDFs.
• Very large files (100+ MB) may hit browser memory limits.

But for 95% of everyday PDF tasks, this toolkit does the job without asking you to hand over your files.
